# Licensing Model

NNLM is **not an open source project**.

The source is available for transparency, evaluation, and collaboration — but **use is permissioned**.

This document explains how licensing works at a conceptual level.
It does not replace the actual license terms.

---

## Source-Available, Not Open Source

NNLM follows a **source-available licensing model**, consistent with NAM.

This means:

* you can read the code
* you can understand how the system works
* you can evaluate its architecture and behavior

It does *not* mean:

* unrestricted use
* free commercial deployment
* automatic rights to modify or redistribute

---

## Why Source-Available

NNLM is a novel architecture. Its value comes from:

* structural grounding guarantees
* the relationship between pipeline stages
* the coupling with NAM as a memory substrate
* the training methodology and architectural decisions

Source availability enables honest evaluation.
Permissioned use ensures the system is deployed as intended.

---

## Evaluation Licenses

Evaluation licenses may be granted for:

* technical assessment
* research exploration
* proof-of-concept work
* partner evaluation

These licenses are:

* time-bounded
* scope-limited
* non-transferable

They do not grant rights to deploy NNLM in production environments.

---

## Commercial Use

Commercial use of NNLM requires a **separate commercial license**.

Commercial licensing terms may vary based on:

* deployment scale
* use case
* whether NNLM is embedded or standalone
* support and collaboration requirements

Commercial licenses are negotiated directly.

---

## Relationship to NAM Licensing

NNLM requires NAM as its memory substrate.

Licensing NNLM does not automatically grant a NAM license.
Both must be licensed appropriately for any deployment.

This is consistent with the architectural reality: NNLM and NAM are co-dependent.
Their licensing reflects that co-dependency.

---

## Modifications and Derivative Work

Modifying NNLM does not grant ownership or usage rights.

Unless explicitly agreed:

* derivative works remain subject to the original license
* modified versions may not be redistributed
* architectural constraints must be preserved

Forking for learning or evaluation is permitted.
Forking to compete or bypass licensing is not.

---

## Why This Model Exists

NNLM explores a new category of system — structurally grounded language generation.

This licensing model exists to:

* enable serious evaluation
* support responsible deployment
* maintain architectural coherence
* allow sustainable development

It is not intended to block use — only to ensure use is aligned.
